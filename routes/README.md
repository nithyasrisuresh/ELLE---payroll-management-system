## 📌 About
ELLE is a full-stack payroll management system built to simplify and automate HR and payroll operations for organizations. It supports multi-department management, employee records, attendance tracking, and payroll generation.

## ✨ Features
- 🔐 Role-based Login (Admin / HR / Employee)
- 👥 Employee Management (Add, Edit, Delete)
- 🏢 Department Management (34 departments across 6 domains)
- 💰 Payroll Generation with allowances & deductions
- 📅 Attendance Tracking (Present / Absent / Leave)
- 📊 Reports & Payslip Generation
